World Resort: privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who is responsible

World Resort is an iPhone game by Carlos Sáez Alfonsea. Contact: info@chesscorrector.com.

No account, nothing on our servers

The game asks for no sign-up, e-mail or name. Your progress (saved games, level, tasks, postcards, rolls and rerolls) and your settings stay on your iPhone only and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes them. We send no usage statistics to any server of ours.

Game Center (Apple)

Online games with friends and leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you sign in, Apple processes your Game Center identifier and nickname, and the other players in your games see your nickname and score. The game in progress travels between phones through Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases ("No ads", roll packs and reroll packs) are charged and handled by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only receives confirmation of what you bought. Nothing sold is random.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and paid for by ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited): a full-screen ad between games and optional ads you choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure them, Google may process device data such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, model and system, and how you interact with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, with Google's form (IAB TCF). If you do not consent, ads are not personalised with your data. Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt.

You can change your choice at any time in the game's Shop › Ad privacy, and in iOS Settings › Privacy › Tracking. The "No ads" purchase removes the ads between games.

How Google uses these data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Legal basis

Your consent for personalised advertising and for accessing the device to that end. Legitimate interest for showing non-personalised ads, measuring them and preventing fraud, under Google's terms. Performance of the contract for in-app purchases.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Children

World Resort is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, write to us.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability by writing to info@chesscorrector.com. As we keep no game data, what we can act on concerns advertising; for data processed by Google or Apple you can also contact them. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Changes

If we change this policy we will say so here, with the date. If a change affects how data are processed, you will see it in the game.
